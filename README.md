## 🚀 About UNIVO

**UNIVO** is a student-focused social platform designed to bring university students together in one digital space.

The platform is built around the everyday student experience, helping students **connect, communicate, learn, discover opportunities and make student life easier.**

UNIVO brings social networking, student communities, messaging, learning tools, resources and student services together in one platform.

---

## 🌍 Our Vision

Our goal is to build UNIVO into one of the leading student-focused mobile platforms in **Zambia 🇿🇲** and eventually expand beyond Zambia.

We are building more than an application.

We are building a digital ecosystem where students can connect with their communities, find opportunities, communicate with other students and access useful tools throughout their academic journey.

> **Connect. Learn. Grow.**

UNIVO Study AI is designed to help students understand academic content and make studying easier.

Students can use it to:

- Ask questions
- Explain concepts
- Summarize information
- Analyze documents
- Analyze supported images
- Review previous conversations
- Create new study conversations

## 📱 Android Application

UNIVO is available as a native Android application.

The Android application connects to the existing UNIVO platform and backend infrastructure rather than operating as a separate isolated system.
