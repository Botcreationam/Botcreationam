# 🚀 About UNIVO

**UNIVO** is a student-focused social platform designed to bring university students together in one digital space.

UNIVO is built around the everyday student experience, helping students connect, communicate, learn, discover opportunities and access useful student services.

The platform brings different parts of student life together in one place.

### UNIVO is built for:

- 🎓 University students
- 👥 Student communities
- 🏫 Universities and institutions
- 📚 Academic learning
- 💬 Student communication
- 🛒 Student marketplace activity
- 🏠 Accommodation discovery
- 🤝 Student networking
- 📅 Events and activities

> **Connect • Learn • Grow.**

---

# 🌍 Our Vision

The vision behind UNIVO is to build one of the leading student-focused digital platforms in Zambia 🇿🇲.

The goal is not simply to create another social media application.

The goal is to create a digital ecosystem around student life.

A platform where students can:

```text
CONNECT
   ↓
COMMUNICATE
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
DISCOVER
   ↓
GROW
UNIVO starts with students in Zambia with a long-term vision of expanding beyond Zambia.
